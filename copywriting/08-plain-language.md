# Rule 08: Write at a grade 5 to 7 reading level

## What it is

Use short sentences, everyday words and active voice. Aim for copy a 12-year-old could follow. This is about clarity, not about talking down.

## Why it works

Unbounce's 2024 benchmark of 41,000+ landing pages found:

| Reading level | Median conversion rate |
| ------------- | ---------------------- |
| Grade 5 to 7  | 11.1%                  |
| Grade 8 to 9  | 7.1%                   |
| Professional  | 5.3%                   |

Difficult words, reading time and word count all correlated negatively with conversion. Financial services was the one industry where more complex language could help. This is vendor data and a correlation: simpler pages may also be simpler offers.

Government style guides agree on the mechanics. GOV.UK asks for sentences under 25 words, no more than five sentences per paragraph, and everyday words: "buy" not "purchase", "help" not "assist". It cites research that 14-word sentences give over 90% comprehension and 43-word sentences under 10%. GOV.UK also notes specialists prefer plain English, with 80% preferring it in one legal-language study.

## Thresholds

| Measure                                   | Target                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Average sentence length                   | 15 to 20 words                        |
| Longest sentence                          | about 25 words                        |
| Paragraph                                 | 5 sentences or fewer, under 150 words |
| Reading level (Hemingway, Flesch-Kincaid) | grade 5 to 7 for most pages           |
| Voice                                     | active ("we send", not "is sent")     |

## Bad

> Utilising our proprietary methodology, clients are enabled to facilitate the optimisation of their operational workflows, thereby achieving enhanced efficiency outcomes.

## Good

> We find the slow steps in how your team works and fix them. Most clients save a day a week.

## Fix

Paste the copy into a readability tool. Split any sentence over 25 words. Swap long words for short ones: use for utilise, help for facilitate, start for commence, about for regarding. Turn passive sentences active.

Source: [Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report (2024)](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/?trk=s-bl), [GOV.UK: clear language](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-language/), [Inside GOV.UK: why 25 words is our limit (2014)](https://insidegovuk.blog.gov.uk/2014/08/04/sentence-length-why-25-words-is-our-limit/), [Digital.gov: clear and short](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/writing/clear-short)

Evidence: vendor and correlational for conversion (Unbounce); strong official guidance for readability (GOV.UK, Digital.gov).
