# Rule 22: Homepage: who you are, what you do, why you

## What it is

A homepage answers three questions in the first screen: who you are, what you do and why someone should choose you. Then it shows real examples and sends each type of visitor to the right place.

## Why it works

NN/g's 2024 homepage principles:

- Logo top-left, not centred.
- A concise tagline saying what you do, unless the company name already says it.
- A value proposition in the hero: why choose you over the alternatives.
- Real examples of what you offer, not category labels.
- Specific link labels.
- No animated headlines, decorative stock photos or needless pop-ups.
- No "Welcome to..." and no jargon.

NN/g's About Us research found that journalists, investors and ordinary users sometimes couldn't tell what a company did from its own site.

## Section order

1. Logo, navigation, one primary CTA
2. Tagline: what you do and for whom
3. Value proposition: why you
4. Real examples (products, projects, services with outcomes)
5. Proof (logos, numbers, testimonials)
6. Routes for each audience ("For clinics", "For hospitals")
7. Final CTA

## Self-check (from Wynter's message-testing questions, useful for B2B)

- Does the headline make the buyer feel understood?
- What's confusing?
- Which objections go unanswered?
- Why would they choose you over the alternatives?

## Bad

> Welcome to Brightline. Powering your digital journey.
> [Stock photo of people pointing at a laptop]
> Our Services | Our Solutions | Our Values

## Good

> Brightline builds booking and payment systems for clinics in Australia and the Gulf.
> Patients book online in Arabic or English. You get paid on the day.
> [Screenshot of the booking screen]
> Used by 60 clinics in Kuwait and Sydney. [Book a demo]

(Illustrative.)

## Fix

Write the tagline as a plain sentence a stranger could repeat. Replace every stock photo with a screenshot, real photo or result. Replace every category label ("Solutions") with a specific one ("Online booking for clinics").
