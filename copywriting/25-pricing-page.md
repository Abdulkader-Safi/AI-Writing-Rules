# Rule 25: Pricing page: answer the billing questions first

## What it is

A pricing page should leave no question about money unanswered: what each plan costs, who it's for, what's included, when you're charged, how to cancel and how refunds work.

## Why it works

No primary research on pricing-page copy turned up (NN/g, CXL and Paddle sources couldn't be retrieved). The best evidence is indirect:

- Crazy Egg (Conversion Rate Experts): explaining the trial, why a card is needed and how yearly billing works lifted sign-ups 116%.
- Highrise (2009): headlines leading with the free trial and "no hidden fees" won.
- Moz: an unexplained $1 trial drew about 500 "what's the catch?" messages.

The pattern: unanswered money questions stop people.

## Structure (convention)

- Label each plan by who it suits ("For solo consultants", "For teams of 5 to 50"), not just "Basic, Pro, Enterprise"
- Price per month and per year, stated plainly, with the saving in real money
- What's included, with the main differences between plans first
- Trial terms and card requirement
- When the first charge happens
- How to cancel, how to change plans, how refunds work
- FAQ on the page itself
- A "most popular" badge only if it's true

## Bad

> Basic $19 | Pro $49 | Enterprise Contact us
> [Long grid of 40 ticks and crosses]
> [Buy now]

## Good

> **Solo** for freelancers. $19/month, or $190/year (2 months free).
> **Team** for 2 to 20 people. $49/month. Most popular with agencies.
> **Business** for 20+. From $199/month, with onboarding help.
>
> All plans: 14-day free trial, no card. Cancel online any time. If you cancel within 30 days of paying, we refund you in full.

(Illustrative.)

## Fix

List every money question your sales team or support inbox gets. Answer each on the pricing page in one or two sentences. Put the main differences between plans at the top of each column, not buried in a grid.

Source: [Conversion Rate Experts: Crazy Egg](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/crazy-egg-case-study/), [Signal v. Noise: Highrise headline tests (2009)](https://signalvnoise.com/posts/1525-writing-decisions-headline-tests-on-the-highrise-signup-page), [Conversion Rate Experts: Moz](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/moz-case-study/)

Evidence: thin. Convention built on indirect checkout results.
