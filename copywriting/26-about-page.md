# Rule 26: About page: layered, real, backed by outsiders

## What it is

The About page answers "who are these people and can I trust them?" It works best in layers, from a one-line summary down to detail, with real people and outside proof.

## Why it works

NN/g's 2019 About Us research (three rounds, 70+ users, 100 sites) recommends four layers:

1. A tagline on the homepage
2. A 1 to 2 paragraph summary of goals and main achievements
3. Sections or subpages for mission, history, team, careers and news
4. About links in the footer

Users wanted authenticity:

- Stock photos, walls of text and aggressive sales tactics lowered satisfaction.
- Real photos, easy contact options and endorsements from reputable outsiders raised it.
- Reviews came up more than 40 times across 20 sessions.

## Structure for a small business (one page)

1. What we do, in one sentence
2. A short summary: who it's for, how long, what you've achieved
3. The team: real photos, names, roles, one line each
4. Why trust us: client logos, awards, reviews, certifications
5. How to reach us

## Bad

> Founded on a passion for excellence, our journey began with a simple vision: to transform the way businesses connect with the world. Our values of integrity, innovation and impact guide everything we do...
> [Stock photo of a handshake]

## Good

> dsrpt builds websites and apps for businesses in Australia and the Gulf. We've been doing it since 2015, from offices in Sydney and Kuwait City.
>
> [Real team photo] Safi, lead engineer. Builds the systems behind client sites.
>
> Clients include KIF and KDIPA. 4.9 on Google from 37 reviews.
>
> [Talk to us] or call +965 ...

(Illustrative; use only real facts.)

## Fix

Cut every sentence about values that has no fact behind it. Replace stock photos with real ones. Add at least one piece of outside proof (reviews, press, client logos with permission).

Source: [NN/g: About Us information on websites (2019)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/about-us-information-on-websites/)

Evidence: strong but old (NN/g, 2019).
