# Rule 27: Case studies: a named customer, a dated result

## What it is

A case study tells the story of one real customer: who they are, the problem, what you did and what changed, with a specific result and a quote from a named person.

## Why it works

No authoritative source on case-study structure was found. The common order (challenge, solution, results) is convention. What the evidence supports is specificity and honesty:

- Users compare your testimonials against third-party reviews (NN/g).
- The FTC rule bans invented or altered testimonials (Rule 17).
- Specific, dated results read as more believable. Copyhackers uses results like "I closed a $50K project [Q3, 2024]".

Conversion Rate Experts ran a customer case-study contest for Crazy Egg to collect real stories in customers' own words.

## Structure (convention)

1. Headline with the result: "How Northwind cut onboarding from 6 weeks to 9 days"
2. The customer: who, what size, where
3. The problem, in their words
4. What they tried before (the alternative)
5. What you did, briefly
6. The result, with numbers and a time frame
7. A quote from a named person
8. CTA for readers with the same problem

## Bad

> Client X came to us needing a digital transformation. We delivered a holistic solution that exceeded expectations. "Great team!" - Client X

## Good

> **Bumper to Bumper: 16 branches, one booking system, 6 weeks**
> Bumper to Bumper runs 16 car service branches in Kuwait. Customers booked by phone, and each branch kept its own diary, so double bookings were common.
> We built one bilingual booking system for all branches.
> In the first three months, phone calls dropped 40% and online bookings reached 1,200 a month.
> "Our staff answer the phone half as often, and the bookings are right." - Fahad, operations manager

(Illustrative; use only real, permissioned figures and quotes.)

## Fix

Interview the customer with specific questions: what was happening before, what did you try, what changed, by how much, how long did it take? Get written permission for the quote and the numbers. Put the result in the headline.
