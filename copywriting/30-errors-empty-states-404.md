# Rule 30: Error messages, empty states and 404 pages

## What it is

The small screens people see when something goes wrong or there's nothing to show yet. The copy should say what happened, without blame, and what to do next. Keep it short.

## Why it works

NN/g's error-message guidelines (2023):

- Put the message next to the problem.
- Don't rely on colour alone.
- Use plain language, no error codes.
- Describe the specific problem and suggest a fix.
- Avoid blaming words like "invalid" or "illegal".
- Keep what the user already typed.
- Skip humour that goes stale on the second visit.

NN/g on empty states (2021): show the system status, teach in context and offer a direct action.

For 404 pages no primary source was found. The same principles apply by inference.

## Bad

Error:

> Invalid input. Error code 0x45.

Empty state:

> No data.

404:

> 404. Oops! You broke the internet!

## Good

Error:

> That email is missing an @. Check it and try again.

Empty state:

> No saved searches yet. Tap the star on any search to save it here.

404:

> We can't find that page. It may have moved. Try searching, or go to [Services], [Pricing] or the [homepage].

## Fix

Trigger every error your forms and app can produce. Rewrite each as: what happened + how to fix it. For each empty screen, add one line on what will appear there and a button to make it happen. Give the 404 page a search box and your three most visited links.

Source: [NN/g: error-message guidelines (2023)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/error-message-guidelines/), [NN/g: empty state design (2021)](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/)
