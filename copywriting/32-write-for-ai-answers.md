# Rule 32: Answer the question in the first sentence under each heading

## What it is

AI answers (Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity) pull short passages from pages and show them before the links. Fewer people click through. Copy that states the answer plainly under a clear heading is easier to quote and still useful to the people who do arrive.

## Why it matters

- Ahrefs (Feb 2026) compared 300,000 keywords in December 2023 and December 2025. When an AI Overview appeared, the top result's click-through rate was 58% lower. Its April 2025 study had found 34.5%. Ahrefs is an SEO vendor.
- Pew Research (July 2025): users clicked a traditional result on about 8% of visits with an AI summary, against 15% without. Reported secondhand.

Google says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet. No special files or schema are needed. No official guidance from OpenAI or Perplexity on how they choose sources was found, so treat vendor "GEO" tactics as speculation.

## Rules

- Use headings that match real questions ("How much does a clinic website cost in Kuwait?").
- Put the direct answer in the first sentence under the heading.
- Make each section make sense on its own, without "as mentioned above".
- State facts plainly with numbers, dates and names.
- Show real expertise: who wrote it, from what experience.

## Bad

> ## Pricing considerations
>
> There are many factors that can influence the overall cost of a website, and it's important to consider each of them carefully before making a decision...

## Good

> ## How much does a clinic website cost in Kuwait?
>
> Most clinic websites with online booking cost KWD 1,500 to 4,000 to build, plus KWD 20 to 60 a month for hosting and booking software. The main cost drivers are the number of pages, Arabic translation and the booking integration.

(Illustrative; use real figures.)

## Fix

Turn each section heading into the question it answers. Move the answer to the first sentence. Read each section alone: does it still make sense? This is the same habit that serves human scanners (Rule 06).

Source: [Google: AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Ahrefs: AI Overviews reduce clicks (2026)](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/), [Search Engine Roundtable on Pew (2025)](https://www.seroundtable.com/pew-google-ai-overviews-39808.html)

Evidence: strong for Google's own statements; vendor and secondhand for the click data. No evidence for specific GEO tactics.
