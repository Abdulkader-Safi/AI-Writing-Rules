# Rule 35: AI drafts need a human edit before they go live

## What it is

AI tools can draft website copy fast. Left unedited, the drafts tend toward generic claims and padding, plus patterns readers recognise as AI. A person has to check every fact and cut the filler.

## Why it matters

Klaviyo's 2026 survey of 8,000 adults in eight countries (December 2025) found that among people who notice AI-generated brand content, 31% trust the brand less and 7% trust it more. That's vendor data on self-reported feelings, not conversion.

No controlled test of AI-written against human-written website copy was found. But the conversion evidence rewards exactly what unedited AI drafts lack: customer language (Rule 01), specific claims (Rule 09) and short, plain copy (Rules 07 and 08).

## Where AI helps

- Summarising review mining and survey answers into themes
- Generating many headline and CTA variants to choose from
- Rewriting to a lower reading level
- Checking a draft against this rule set

## Where AI hurts

- Inventing figures or testimonials (a legal risk, see Rule 17)
- Writing generic claims any competitor could use
- Adding hype words and padding
- Producing patterns readers recognise as AI: see the [research/](../skills/anti-ai-writing/research/README.md) folder for the full list

## Bad

Asking "write a homepage for my web agency" and publishing the result:

> Elevate your digital presence with our cutting-edge solutions. We're not just a web agency, we're your partner in growth.

## Good

Giving the AI the positioning sentence, 20 customer phrases, the objection list and real figures. Asking for ten headline options. Picking one, then editing it by hand:

> Websites for Kuwait clinics, live in 6 weeks, with online booking in Arabic and English.

## Fix

Feed AI tools your research, never just a topic. Check every number and name against a source. Run the draft through the anti-AI writing rules and the slop checker. Read it aloud. If it could be on a competitor's site, rewrite it.

Source: [eMarketer on Klaviyo's 2026 consumer report](https://www.emarketer.com/content/visible-ai-marketing-four-times-more-likely-cost-brands-trust-than-build)

Evidence: vendor survey data on trust. No conversion test found.
