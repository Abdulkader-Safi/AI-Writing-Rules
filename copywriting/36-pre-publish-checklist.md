# Rule 36: Pre-publish checklist

Run this before any page goes live. Each item links to the rule behind it.

## Message and structure

- [ ] A visitor who reads only the headline knows what you sell and who it's for ([04](04-headline-says-what-you-sell.md))
- [ ] The subhead says why they should care, in roughly 10 to 13 words ([05](05-subhead-says-why.md))
- [ ] The main offer and primary CTA are visible without scrolling ([04](04-headline-says-what-you-sell.md))
- [ ] The opening matches the traffic's awareness stage ([03](03-awareness-stage.md))
- [ ] Each heading starts with its most informative words and makes sense alone ([06](06-write-for-the-scan.md))
- [ ] The first sentence of each section answers its heading ([32](32-write-for-ai-answers.md))
- [ ] The page asks for one main action ([21](21-one-page-one-action.md))

## Words

- [ ] Customer phrases from reviews, surveys or calls appear in the copy ([01](01-research-before-writing.md))
- [ ] Sentences average 15 to 20 words, none over about 25 ([08](08-plain-language.md))
- [ ] Paragraphs are five sentences or fewer ([08](08-plain-language.md))
- [ ] No hype words, no unexplained jargon, no "Welcome to..." ([07](07-cut-the-hype.md))
- [ ] Every claim can be pictured, proved false, and isn't something a competitor could say ([09](09-specific-claims.md))
- [ ] The copy is about the reader more than about you ([10](10-you-not-we.md))
- [ ] The tone fits the moment: calm on errors and pricing ([11](11-voice-and-tone.md))

## Conversion

- [ ] Every major objection from research has an answer, with proof beside it ([12](12-answer-objections.md))
- [ ] CTA buttons say what the visitor gets, never "Submit" ([13](13-cta-names-the-outcome.md))
- [ ] Trial, card, billing, cancellation and refund terms sit near the CTA ([14](14-lower-the-commitment.md))
- [ ] Every form field is necessary; any odd field explains why it's there ([15](15-form-microcopy.md))
- [ ] Optional fields are marked; errors say exactly what to fix ([15](15-form-microcopy.md))
- [ ] On long pages, the CTA repeats after major sections ([19](19-length-matches-objections.md))

## Proof and legal

- [ ] Testimonials are specific and placed next to the claim they support ([16](16-specific-social-proof.md))
- [ ] Every testimonial is real, unchanged in meaning, permissioned and on file ([17](17-honest-proof-and-the-law.md))
- [ ] Incentives and relationships behind reviews are disclosed ([17](17-honest-proof-and-the-law.md))
- [ ] Customer counts, logos and "most popular" badges are true and current ([17](17-honest-proof-and-the-law.md))
- [ ] Any deadline or stock limit is real; no resetting timers or pre-ticked add-ons ([18](18-honest-urgency.md))

## SEO and accessibility

- [ ] The page title is unique and descriptive, important words first ([31](31-seo-copy.md))
- [ ] The meta description summarises this page with a useful fact ([31](31-seo-copy.md))
- [ ] Headings follow a real H1, H2, H3 structure ([33](33-accessible-copy.md))
- [ ] Link text describes the destination; no "click here" ([33](33-accessible-copy.md))
- [ ] Informative images have alt text; decorative ones have empty alt ([33](33-accessible-copy.md))
- [ ] Abbreviations are spelled out on first use ([33](33-accessible-copy.md))
- [ ] At least one plain-text internal link points to and from this page ([31](31-seo-copy.md))

## Final read

- [ ] Read on a phone, top to bottom ([34](34-test-copy-properly.md))
- [ ] Cut by a third and check whether anything was lost ([07](07-cut-the-hype.md))
- [ ] If AI helped, a person has checked every fact and removed the padding ([35](35-edit-ai-drafts.md))
