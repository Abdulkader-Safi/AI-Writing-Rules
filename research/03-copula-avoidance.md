# Pattern 03: Dodging "is" and "are"

## What it is

Refusing plain statements of fact. Instead of saying what something is, the sentence dresses it up.

## The substitutions

- "serves as" instead of is
- "stands as" instead of is
- "functions as" instead of is
- "represents" instead of is
- "operates as" instead of is
- "marks" instead of is
- "boasts", "features", "maintains", "offers" instead of has

Also: "ventured into politics as a candidate" instead of "was a candidate". "Began his career as an engineer" instead of "was an engineer".

## Why it happens

"Is" reads as flat to a model tuned for prose that sounds considered. Verbs of standing and serving fill the same slot with more ceremony.

## Bad

> The library serves as a lightweight alternative to Redux and boasts a bundle size of 2kb.

## Good

> The library is a lightweight alternative to Redux. It is 2kb.

## Fix

Whenever you see one of these verbs, try substituting "is" or "has". If the sentence still works, it was padding.
