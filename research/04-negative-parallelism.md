# Pattern 04: Negative parallelism

## What it is
The most recognisable AI sentence shape. Three variants:

1. **"Not just X, but Y"** and "Not only X but also Y"
2. **"It's not X, it's Y"** and the stacked version "No X, no Y, just Z"
3. **"X rather than Y"**, common in Grok output

## Why it happens
The construction manufactures an insight by inventing a misconception and then correcting it. It reads as a reveal while adding nothing. Models learned it from marketing copy, LinkedIn posts, and self-help writing, all heavily represented in training data.

## Threshold
Three or more in one article means templating, not style.

## Bad
> It's not just a note-taking app, it's a second brain.
> This isn't about working harder. It's about working smarter.
> We focused on clarity rather than cleverness.

## Good
> It stores notes and links them to each other.
> We cut the meeting from an hour to twenty minutes.
> We wrote it plainly.

## Fix
Delete the negation and keep only the positive half. If the positive half cannot stand on its own, the sentence had no content. The negated thing is usually a strawman nobody claimed.
