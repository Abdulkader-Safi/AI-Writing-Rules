# Pattern 05: Rule of three on autopilot

## What it is

Lists that always contain exactly three items, usually with matched rhythm and matched grammatical shape. Called a tricolon.

## Why it happens

Three is the shortest count that reads as a complete survey. Models default to it because it sounds comprehensive without committing to a real inventory. Marketing copy in training data is saturated with it.

## Threshold

More than one polished triplet per 200 words.

## Bad

> The system is fast, reliable, and scalable.
> Plan, execute, and optimize.
> It saves time, cuts cost, and improves morale.

## Good

> The system handles 4,000 requests a second.
> Plan it, then ship it.
> It cut our deploy time from 20 minutes to 90 seconds.

## Fix

Count the items in every list. If they are all three, break the pattern: use one strong word, or two, or four. Make the count reflect the real number of things, not the rhythm.

Watch for it in headings and section counts too, not only in sentences. Three feature bullets, three steps, three benefits.

Source: [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [SlopDetector](https://slopdetector.org/blog/signs-of-ai-writing)
