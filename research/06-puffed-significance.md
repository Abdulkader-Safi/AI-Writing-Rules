# Pattern 06: Puffed-up significance

## What it is

Inflating a mundane subject by tying it to grand themes with vague language.

## Watch phrases

stands as, serves as, is a testament to, plays a crucial role, plays a pivotal role, plays a vital role, underscores the importance of, reflects a broader, symbolizing, contributing to, setting the stage for, marks a significant milestone, a key turning point, the evolving landscape of, left an indelible mark, deeply rooted in, in the broader context of

## Why it happens

Models are tuned to produce writing that feels weighty. Significance claims are cheap to generate and impossible to verify, so they fill space without risk.

## Bad

> The 2019 rebrand stands as a testament to the company's commitment to innovation and marks a pivotal moment in the evolving landscape of consumer fintech.

## Good

> They rebranded in 2019. Signups went up 12% in the following quarter.

## Fix

State what happened. Delete every clause explaining why it matters to the universe. If significance is real, name a consequence with a number or a date attached.
