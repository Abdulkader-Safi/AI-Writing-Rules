# Pattern 07: Trailing "-ing" commentary

## What it is

A sentence states a fact, then bolts on a participial phrase that comments vaguely on it. Wikipedia calls this superficial analysis.

## Watch phrases

highlighting, underscoring, emphasizing, ensuring, reflecting, symbolizing, contributing to, cultivating, fostering, encompassing, enhancing, showcasing, aligning with, resonating with, allowing for, paving the way for

## Why it happens

The clause gives the appearance of analysis without any claim that could be checked. It also inflates word count on demand.

## Bad

> The team shipped the update in March, highlighting their commitment to reliability and ensuring a smoother experience for users.

## Good

> The team shipped the update in March. Crash reports dropped by half.

## Fix

Cut everything after the comma. If the trailing clause carried a real claim, promote it to its own sentence and give it evidence.

Source: [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)
