# Pattern 08: Vague attribution

## What it is
Claims sourced to an unnamed authority.

## Watch phrases
experts say, experts agree, studies have shown, research suggests, industry reports indicate, observers have cited, some critics argue, it is widely regarded, many believe, several sources note, it has been said

Also: "such as" placed in front of a list that is actually exhaustive, implying there is more.

## Why it happens
The model has a diffuse impression from training data but no specific citation. Vague attribution converts that impression into something that sounds sourced.

## Threshold
More than half the authority claims in a piece being uncited.

## Bad
> Experts agree that remote work improves retention, and studies have shown a significant productivity gain.

## Good
> A 2023 Stanford study of 1,600 workers found a 13% productivity gain for remote staff.

Or, if you do not have the source:

> Remote work seems to help retention, though I have not seen good numbers on it.

## Fix
Name the source or drop the claim. Hedging honestly in your own voice beats borrowing authority you cannot produce.
