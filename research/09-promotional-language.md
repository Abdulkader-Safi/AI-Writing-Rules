# Pattern 09: Promotional language

## What it is
Travel-brochure tone applied to any subject, including ones with no reason to be sold.

## Watch phrases
boasts a, vibrant, rich, profound, enhancing, showcasing, exemplifies, a commitment to, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, seamless experience, world-class, best-in-class, enterprise-grade, empowering users to, unlock the potential of, supercharge, streamline, revolutionize, game-changing

## Why it happens
Marketing copy is a huge share of the web text models train on, and the register bleeds into everything.

## Bad
> Nestled in the heart of the city, this vibrant cafe boasts a rich selection of world-class coffee, empowering guests to enjoy a seamless morning experience.

## Good
> The cafe is on Bourke Street. They roast their own beans and open at 6am.

## Fix
For every adjective, ask what fact sits behind it. Replace the adjective with the fact. "World-class coffee" becomes "they roast their own beans."
