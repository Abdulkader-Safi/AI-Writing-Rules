# Pattern 10: Empty-opener cliches

## What it is

First sentences that set a scene instead of saying anything.

## Watch phrases

In today's fast-paced world, In the digital age, In an era of, Picture this, Imagine a world where, We live in a time when, Now more than ever, It's no secret that, In recent years, As technology continues to evolve, Let's face it

## Why it happens

Models are trained to produce a warm-up before the substance. Blog-post training data almost always has one.

## Threshold

Two or more in a 500-word piece.

## Bad

> In today's fast-paced digital world, businesses are increasingly turning to automation.

## Good

> We automated invoice chasing in March. It saved about six hours a week.

## Fix

Delete the first sentence. Then check whether the second one also needs deleting. Start with the fact, the number, or the thing that happened.
