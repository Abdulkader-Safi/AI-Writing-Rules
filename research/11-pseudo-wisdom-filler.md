# Pattern 11: Pseudo-wisdom filler

## What it is
Sentences that sound like insight and carry none. They pass every grammar and topicality check while saying nothing that could be false.

## Examples
- The key is to find the right balance.
- Success ultimately comes down to consistency.
- At the end of the day, it depends on your specific needs.
- True growth comes from within.
- Context is everything.
- There is no one-size-fits-all answer.
- The best approach is the one that works for you.

## Why it happens
These sentences are the highest-probability continuation almost anywhere. They are safe, agreeable, and unfalsifiable.

## Test
The deletion test. Remove the sentence. If nothing is lost, it was filler.

## Threshold
More than a third of sentences surviving deletion with no loss of meaning.

## Fix
Delete, or replace with the specific version. "It depends on your needs" becomes "if you have under 50 users, use the free tier."
