# Pattern 14: Thin punctuation

## What it is

Prose that leans on commas and "and" while avoiding semicolons, colons, parentheses, and full stops in the middle of a thought.

## Why it happens

Models generate smooth, additive clauses. Punctuation that signals a shift in register, an aside, or an interruption is a human move and appears less often.

The exception is the em dash, which models overuse instead. See pattern 15.

## Bad

> The migration took three weeks and involved rewriting the data layer and we also had to update all the client code and the tests.

## Good

> The migration took three weeks: rewriting the data layer, updating every client, then the tests (which took longer than the rewrite).

## Fix

Look for chains of "and". Break them with a colon, a full stop, or brackets. Use a semicolon when two independent statements belong in one breath.

Source: [How to spot AI writing in 2026](https://aiadventureclub.substack.com/p/how-to-spot-ai-writing-in-2026)
