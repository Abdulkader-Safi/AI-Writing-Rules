# Pattern 20: Outline-shaped endings

## What it is
The wrap-up section that exists because the outline had a slot for it.

## The shapes
- "Challenges and future prospects"
- "Despite its [positive adjectives], [subject] faces several challenges..."
- "In conclusion,"
- "As we look ahead,"
- "Moving forward,"
- "Only time will tell"
- A closing paragraph that restates the opening paragraph
- Section summaries recapping what the reader just read

The classic formula: a challenges paragraph, then vague optimism about future initiatives, then a sentence about the broader significance.

## Why it happens
Blog posts and essays in training data almost always have a conclusion. The model produces one whether or not there is anything left to say.

## Bad
> In conclusion, while the framework faces challenges around adoption and tooling, its future looks promising as the ecosystem continues to evolve. Only time will tell how it shapes the broader landscape.

## Good
End at the last real sentence. Delete the conclusion.

## Fix
Cut the final paragraph. Read what is left. It almost always ends better.

If the piece genuinely needs a close, make it a specific next action or an open question you actually have.
