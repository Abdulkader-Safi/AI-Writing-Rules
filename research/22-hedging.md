# Pattern 22: Hedging everything

## What it is
Stacked qualifiers that drain a claim of any content.

## Watch phrases
it could be argued that, some might say, in many cases, generally speaking, it is often the case that, may potentially, can sometimes, arguably, to some extent, in certain contexts, tends to, is likely to, one could reasonably conclude

The tell is stacking. One hedge is honest. Three in a sentence is a refusal to claim anything.

## Why it happens
Models are tuned to avoid being wrong. Hedging is the cheapest way to be unfalsifiable.

## Bad
> It could be argued that, in many cases, this approach may potentially offer some degree of improvement, at least in certain contexts.

## Good
> This is faster in most cases. It is slower when the dataset is under 100 rows.

Or, honestly:

> I think this is faster. I have not benchmarked it.

## Fix
Make the claim or drop it. If you are genuinely unsure, say so once, in your own voice, and say what would settle it. One honest "I do not know" beats four hedges.
