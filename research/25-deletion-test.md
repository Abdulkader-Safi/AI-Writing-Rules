# Pattern 25: The deletion test

## What it is
The substance check that catches what the vocabulary checks miss. A piece can pass every word-level filter and still say nothing.

## The test
Read a paragraph. Close it. Name one thing you learned: a name, a number, a date, a cause, a trade-off, a decision, a consequence.

If you cannot, the paragraph fails.

## Threshold
More than half the paragraphs failing means the piece is empty regardless of how it reads.

## The variant
Delete each sentence in turn. If the paragraph loses nothing, the sentence was filler. Applied across a draft, this usually cuts 20 to 40 percent.

## Why it matters most
Every other pattern in this folder is a proxy for this one. Vocabulary, tricolons, and hedging are how emptiness disguises itself. This test finds the emptiness directly.

## Bad
> Effective communication is essential for team success. When teams communicate well, they can align on goals and work more efficiently. This is why many organisations invest in improving their communication practices.

Three sentences. Nothing learned.

## Good
> We moved standup from 9am to 2pm because half the team is in Europe. Attendance went from 60% to 95%.

## Fix
For each paragraph that fails, either find the concrete fact behind it or delete the paragraph.
