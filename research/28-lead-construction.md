# Pattern 28: Treating the title as a thing

## What it is

Opening a document by defining its own title as if the title were an object in the world.

## Bad

> List of songs about Mexico is a curated compilation of musical works that reference the country.
> Q3 marketing plan is a comprehensive document outlining our strategy for the third quarter.
> Onboarding checklist serves as a structured guide designed to help new hires get started.

## Why it happens

The model is filling a template: "[title] is a [category] that [purpose]". It works for proper nouns and real entities. It collapses for descriptive titles, because the title is a label rather than a thing.

## Good

> These songs all mention Mexico. Sorted by release year.
> Here is what we are doing in Q3.
> Do these seven things in your first week.

## The general rule

Do not open by explaining what the document is. The reader can see what it is. Open with the first piece of content.

## Fix

Delete the first sentence if it defines the title. Start with the substance.
