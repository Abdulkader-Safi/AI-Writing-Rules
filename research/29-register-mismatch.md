# Pattern 29: Register mismatch

## What it is
One tone applied to every audience and every format. A Slack message written like a whitepaper. A README written like a press release. An email to a friend written like a policy document.

## Why it happens
Models have a default register: formal, complete, balanced, slightly promotional. It is the register that scored well in training. Without explicit direction, everything comes out in it.

## The tells
- A two-line answer arriving as five paragraphs with headings
- Contractions absent where a person would use them
- Full sentences where a person would use a fragment
- Every message opening with a greeting and closing with an offer of further help
- The same warmth level for a bug report and a birthday note

## The related tell: sudden style shift
The reverse case. A document where one section reads differently from the rest, because part of it was generated and part was written. Vocabulary, sentence length, and formality all jump at the seam.

## Fix
Decide the register before writing. For technical readers, go deep and concrete. For non-technical readers, plain English with no assumed knowledge and no jargon left unexplained.

Match the format too. A question that has a one-line answer gets a one-line answer.
